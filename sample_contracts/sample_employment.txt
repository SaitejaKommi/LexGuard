EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2025, between TechCorp Global Inc. ("Company") and the undersigned employee ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Employee agrees to serve in the capacity designated by Company and perform all duties as assigned. Company may unilaterally change Employee's role, responsibilities, or reporting structure at any time without notice or consent.

2. COMPENSATION
Employee shall receive a base salary subject to change at Company's sole discretion. Company reserves the right to reduce compensation by up to 30% without notice and without triggering any termination rights.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
Employee hereby irrevocably assigns to Company ALL intellectual property, inventions, discoveries, and creative works conceived, developed, or reduced to practice by Employee at any time, whether during or outside working hours, whether or not using Company resources, and whether or not related to Company's business. This assignment covers all work created during the term of employment and for a period of FIVE (5) YEARS after termination. Employee waives all moral rights globally and permanently.

4. NON-COMPETE CLAUSE
Employee agrees that for a period of THREE (3) YEARS following termination of employment, for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly:
(a) Work for any company or individual in the same industry anywhere in the world;
(b) Start any business that could compete with Company's current or future business activities;
(c) Consult for, advise, invest in, or assist any competitor;
(d) Solicit any Company customer, partner, or employee.
Violation of this clause entitles Company to seek unlimited injunctive relief and liquidated damages of $500,000 per violation.

5. TERMINATION
Company may terminate this Agreement immediately, without notice and without severance, for any reason or no reason. Employee must provide 90 days written notice to resign. Failure to provide full notice period requires Employee to pay Company 3 months salary as liquidated damages.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employee shall maintain strict confidentiality of all Company information in perpetuity, including information that becomes publicly known through no fault of Employee. This obligation survives termination indefinitely with no sunset clause.

7. MANDATORY ARBITRATION
All disputes shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration conducted by an arbitrator chosen solely by Company. Employee waives all rights to jury trial, class action, and appeal. Arbitration shall be conducted in the Company's headquarters city. Employee shall bear all arbitration costs regardless of outcome.

8. INDEMNIFICATION
Employee shall indemnify and hold harmless Company from any and all claims, damages, or liabilities arising from Employee's work, even if caused by Company's own negligence or breach of law.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction most favorable to Company, as determined solely by Company.

10. MODIFICATION
Company may modify any terms of this Agreement at any time by posting notice on the employee portal. Continued employment constitutes acceptance of modified terms.

Employee acknowledges having read and understood this Agreement and agrees to be bound by all terms herein.
